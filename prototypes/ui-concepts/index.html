Stargaze · five directions

Each page: ↑↓ / ←→ to choose, Enter to go in, Esc to back out. Keys 1–5 switch between the five concepts (also [ and ], or the label at the top).

1 · OrreryBrass and parchment on near-black. Cormorant Garamond + Jost. A living clockwork orrery; field studies sit on its rings. 2 · SignalRadio-telescope brutalism. Black, off-white, one signal orange. Syne + JetBrains Mono. Huge type; a live waveform peaks on whatever you select. 3 · DriftDusk on an alien world. Gradient skies that change per field study, a planet horizon, glowing lights. Sora + Martian Mono. Quiet, centred, almost no chrome. 4 · EyepieceTelescope · You only ever see the world through a round eyepiece, in astronomers' red night-vision light. Michroma + Red Hat Mono. Menu choices are engraved on a focus ring that turns with detents; changing a choice throws the stars out of focus and racks them back. Each field study is a live sky: stars wheel, moons shuttle, suns slip behind the giant. Zooming closes the field stop into an eyepiece with a turning azimuth rim. Corner readouts with keycaps, right-click lock, G targets, and Tab for the theory chart with a live finder. Drag to look, wheel to zoom. 5 · Setting CirclesTelescope · The graduated dials of an equatorial mount. Steel and ice-green on blue-black. Chakra Petch + Azeret Mono. A big dial picks the menu; choosing a field study slews the RA and Dec dials to its coordinates; the observation view has scrolling scales and a 6× finder scope. Drag to look, wheel to zoom.

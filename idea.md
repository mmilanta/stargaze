# Idea

The idea is to replicate the emotions of looking at a night sky with a telescope in a videogame.

The core point is to render a solar system, with all moving parts. And the user can look around from an observatory on a planet and see as time passes all objects.

In particular:

* Stars as background
* Sun
* Moons
* Planets

The idea is to build a rendering engine which is quite flexible. in the future we might want to setup complex solar systemps, maybe with multiple stars, maybe with moons around far away planets and ecplipses renderred by the engine.

Ignore the effect of gravity on light
